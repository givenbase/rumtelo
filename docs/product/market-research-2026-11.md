# Market research and product map — November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-23. Method: five parallel web-research passes (consumer budgeting, all-in-one "life" suites and planning, B2B practices, freelancers, household admin and family), then a spot check of the claims the verdict leans on. Every figure has a source; where a figure came from press or a vendor, it says so. This is desk research. **Nobody has talked to customers for it.**

The product name was never used in a search query.

## 1. Verdict in short

- **The need is real and growing.** 38% of Dutch households struggle to make ends meet (32% in 2024) and a third have regular money worries (Nibud "Geldzaken in de praktijk 2026", via [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/06/most-young-adults-now-struggle-to-make-ends-meet-nibud-finds/)). 27% of zzp'ers have a tax debt ([CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2025/47/ruim-kwart-zelfstandigen-heeft-belastingschuld)).
- **Product-market fit is not proven, and the evidence does not support launching a suite of six to eight products.** Every success found is one sharp product. No all-in-one "money + goals + health + mindset" consumer app was found that scaled.
- **There is a realistic route**: one sharp product (Money + the weekly check, with Planning as the second spoke on the same weekly routine), aimed first at households where private and business money run through each other. The rest of the map is a roadmap earned by retention, not a launch plan.
- **Three things threaten the core today**: banks are giving "split on arrival" away for free, there is no bank sync, and nothing has been validated with real users.

## 2. What the market says

### 2.1 Consumer budgeting

| Finding | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Grip (ABN AMRO) stopped as a standalone app on 16 Dec 2022; features moved into the bank app | [Financer](https://financer.nl/persoonlijke-financien/grip-app-review/) |
| Dyme: 600k Dutch users linked a bank account since 2018, 150k monthly active, first profitable year 2024, sold to insurtech RISK in Sept 2025. Revenue is subscriptions **plus commission on contract switching** | [Peak](https://peak.capital/fintech-startup-dyme-acquired-by-risk/), [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/nieuws/dyme-verkocht-insurtech-risk-miljoenendeal) |
| Monarch (US, budgeting only, ~$100/yr, synced, ad-free): $75M Series B at $850M in May 2025; ~$100M ARR is a Sacra estimate | [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/monarch-money-at-100m-arr/) |
| Mint (free, ad-funded) shut down March 2024; the paid, synced apps took the users | same |
| **ING launched "Inkomen Sparen" on 17 Nov 2026**: a percentage of incoming money is set aside automatically | [Banken.nl](https://www.banken.nl/nieuws/27600/ing-app-wordt-15-en-laat-sparen-meebewegen-met-inkomen) |
| Revolut has an "income sorter"; bunq a "Salary Sorter"; Rabobank up to 20 savings pots and category budgets | [Revolut help](https://help.revolut.com/nl-NL/help/accounts/budget-and-analytics/using-the-income-sorter-feature/), [bunq](https://www.bunq.com/en-es/benefits/budgeting), [Rabobank](https://www.rabobank.nl/particulieren/sparen/spaarpotjes) |
| Freemium apps convert a median 2.1–2.2% of downloads to paid; ~72% of annual subscribers cancel in year one | [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026) |

No Dutch or EU data was found on free→paid conversion or churn for budgeting apps.

**What it means.** "Split first" as a mechanism is no longer unique: a bank can offer one percentage rule for free. What a bank is unlikely to build is the method (six jars with a job each), the weekly routine, the shared household board, and being bank-agnostic. Those four are the product; the split itself is table stakes.

**Bank sync.** The paid winners are all synced. No rigorous study on sync vs. manual retention was found, but manual entry is the obvious dropout risk. Aggregators have moved to sales-led contracts: GoCardless reportedly closed its free tier to new sign-ups in 2025, Enable Banking is free only for private individuals, Tink and Salt Edge quote minimum commitments (sources are thin and partly competitor-written: [Actual Budget docs](https://actualbudget.org/docs/advanced/bank-sync/gocardless/), [Enable Banking FAQ](https://enablebanking.com/docs/faq)). Reading bank data needs an AISP registration with DNB or a licensed partner ([DNB](https://www.dnb.nl/voor-de-sector/open-boek-toezicht-wet-regelgeving/toezicht-wet-regelgeving/psd2/proces-voor-rekeninginformatiedienstaanbieder-om-actief-te-worden)). Budget for quotes before promising it.

**Six jars and IP.** A budgeting method is an idea and not protected; the jar names are generic words. No "JARS" trademark was found, but the search did not reach EUIPO, so this is **not a clearance**. Stay away from "Eker", "Millionaire Mind" and his course text, and have a trademark attorney check before a public launch.

### 2.2 All-in-one "life" suites

- No verified consumer app that bundles money, goals, health and mindset and scaled.
- Demand for a "life OS" shows up as one-off Notion templates ($49–79), not subscriptions ([Thomas Frank](https://typefully.com/TomFrankly/dollar1-million-in-notion-template-sales-kuFT0iD)).
- Focused apps win: Monarch (budget), Finch (self-care), Griply (Dutch goals app, team of four, small but alive: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1556692747)).
- Multi-product companies add products **after** the core is large (Duolingo added maths and music in 2023, [investor release](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-previews-its-multi-subject-future-duocon-2023)); even Notion closed Notion Mail in 2026.
- The link customers do recognise: money stress goes with poor sleep and mood, and less overview and fewer financial goals go with more financial stress (Deloitte/Nibud study via [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/03/young-people-struggling-with-finances-only-12-in-good-health)). That supports **goals next to money**. No evidence was found that people want one app for all of it.

### 2.3 Practice (B2B)

- Demand side is large: 82,120 debt-help requests at NVVK members in 2024 ([Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/sociaal/meer-vraag-naar-schuldhulp-gemiddeld-schuldbedrag-groeit)); ~8.6% of households with registered problematic debt (CBS, via search summary).
- **Buddy Payment** won a tender with 93 municipalities in 2020 and went bankrupt in June 2023 after the municipalities ended the partnership ([Banken.nl](https://www.banken.nl/nieuws/24578/buddy-payment-vraagt-faillissement-aan)). Sources differ on the cause: weak interim results, and critical press about the director's undisclosed stake ([Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/sociaal/bestuursrecht/budgetapp-buddy-failliet)). Either way the business depended on one political buyer.
- Employers pay "a few euros" per employee per month; no uptake data found.
- No count of independent budget coaches exists; no data on what they spend on software.
- The Dutch data-protection authority has said consent is a shaky legal basis in debt help, because a dependent person is not free to refuse ([consultation](https://internetconsultatie.nl/uitwisseling_persoonsgegevens_schuldhulpverlening/document/4368)). Expect the same question about dual consent: keep the consent and revocation log, keep scopes narrow.
- A professional *bewindvoerder* is court-admitted; the access level name "MANAGE" invites confusion with that role.

**What it means.** Municipalities are a poor first buyer for a tiny team. Independent coaches and small offices buying per client are reachable, but unmeasured. Practice works best as a channel: the coach brings the household in, the household stays the customer.

### 2.4 Freelancers

- 1.18M zzp'ers with zzp as main job in Q4 2025, down 95k in a year after enforcement against false self-employment started ([CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/07/aantal-zzp-ers-in-2025-gedaald-met-62-duizend)); the KvK register still shows 1.8M sole proprietorships including side businesses ([Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2026/11/kvk-meer-zzpers-ondanks-aanpak-schijnzelfstandigheid/)).
- 27% have a tax debt, median €1,900 (CBS, above). About 30% set nothing aside for income tax (commercial survey, n=400: [Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2025/3/een-op-de-drie-zzpers-zet-geen-geld-opzij-voor-inkomstenbelasting/)). Only 16% pay for disability insurance ([CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/faq/zzp/hebben-zzp-ers-een-voorziening-voor-arbeidsongeschiktheid-of-pensioen)).
- A sole proprietorship has no legal split between private and business money and cannot pay a salary; money leaves as a private withdrawal ([KvK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/kan-ik-mezelf-salaris-betalen-uit-mijn-eenmanszaak/)). **For this customer, household and business are one wallet.**
- Banks already split business income on arrival: Knab's Inkomstenverdeler (Profit First in all but name, with VAT saving) and bunq AutoBTW ([Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/altijd-winst-dankzij-profit-first-zo-werkt-de-methode), [bunq](https://www.bunq.com/nl-be/blog/zzp-ers-how-much-vat-to-set-aside-and-the-easiest-way-to-do-it)). But only inside one bank, and only the business side.
- No app was found that combines a business split with a household jar system.
- They already pay €10–20 a month for bookkeeping; a €5–15 add-on sits below that. No evidence they pay more than households.
- No forum or survey evidence of people **asking** for one private + business planner. The gap follows from the structure and the tax-debt numbers; it is not demand-validated.

### 2.5 Household admin and family

- People pay for the **saving**, not for storage: Dyme earns commission on switching; comparison sites are commission-funded. Document vaults are free (Doccle, Berichtenbox, iCloud). Paid vaults exist only in the US at $120–480 a year ([Trustworthy](https://www.trustworthy.com/pricing)).
- Real Dutch pain: benefits not claimed (zorgtoeslag ~12% non-use, [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/07/02/dienst-toeslagen-wijst-mensen-op-recht-toeslag)); only 6.4% switched health insurer ([Overstappen.nl](https://www.overstappen.nl/nieuws/aantal-zorgverzekering-overstappers-daalt-naar-6-procent-maar-verschillen-per-gemeente-groot/)).
- Kids' pocket money is free at Dutch banks (bunq, ABN AMRO Gimi, Revolut <18). Nothing was found that links kids' jars to the parents' jar budget.
- Neither is opened weekly. Admin has an annual rhythm; family opens around pocket money only.

**What it means.** Both are features on the Money board (contract end dates and cancel reminders on fixed costs; kids' jars), not standalone paid products on this evidence.

## 3. Risks, in order

| # | Risk | Consequence | What reduces it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nothing validated with real users | Building a suite nobody asked for | 10–15 interviews with zzp households; 20 real households on the weekly check for four weeks |
| 2 | Breadth for a tiny team | Six thin products instead of one that retains | One sharp product; gate every next product on retention of the previous |
| 3 | Banks give the split away | "Split first" alone is not a reason to pay | Sell the method, the routine, the household and bank-agnostic private + business |
| 4 | No bank sync | Manual entry dropout; hard to charge ~€100/yr | CSV first for validation; get aggregator quotes; decide before paid launch |
| 5 | Annual churn (~72% cancel in year one across apps) | Acquisition never pays back | The week check is the retention habit; measure it before anything else |
| 6 | Legal: jar-method branding, consent in coaching, tax "advice" | Rework or a complaint | Trademark check; consent log; user-set percentages labelled as planning, not advice |

## 4. Product map and names

Rule applied: literal, positive, short, and a clean Dutch/English pair. Names are judgment, informed by the research; none has been checked against the BOIP/EUIPO registers.

| # | Product | Recommended name (NL / EN) | Why | Rejected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Overview | **Home / Home** | Known, short | — |
| 2 | Goals, week, today, habits | **Planning / Planning** | Literal and the same word in both languages. "Koers" also means share price or exchange rate, which misleads inside a money app; "Course" reads as a training in English | Kalender/Calendar (means meetings; Google territory), Kompas (metaphor), Doelen (too narrow), Agenda (meeting agenda in English) |
| 3 | Money | **Geld / Money** | Clear | — |
| 4 | Sleep, food, training, rest | **Energie / Energy** | Literal; chakra-like content stays out | — |
| 5 | Learning, intention, gratitude | **Mindset / Mindset** | Same word in both languages; decided 2026-11-22 | Ziel/Soul (spiritual, not literal), Groei/Growth (everything is growth) |
| 6 | Business side | **Bedrijf / Business** | Neutral, works for a sole proprietor | Zaak (an existing platform for entrepreneurs, [dezaak.nl](https://www.dezaak.nl/); also means "case" and "shop"), Zakelijk (adjective, bank-flavoured), Onderneming (formal) |
| 7 | Partner, children, pocket money | **Mensen / People** | Literal and includes singles, couples and flatmates | Gezin (excludes singles), Thuis (clashes with Home), Samen (excludes singles) |
| 8 | Contracts, insurance, deadlines | **Administratie / Admin** | The word people already use for it ("mijn administratie"); neutral, not a burden word | Papieren (also means identity papers; sounds like storage), Kluis (storage; existing products), Geregeld (positive but says nothing alone), Contracten (too narrow) |

In code, `course` stays the key prefix for the planner and `plan` keeps meaning the subscription; "Planning" is a display label only. If that label ever collides with plan/subscription copy, the Dutch subscription word is "abonnement".

## 5. Recommended order

1. **Now — one sharp product.** Money + the weekly check, with Planning beside it because it shares the weekly routine. Rename in the UI: Growth → Planning (top level), Soul → Mindset. Energy and Mindset stay hidden in production.
2. **Validate before building more.** Interviews with 10–15 zzp households; 20 households using the weekly check for four weeks. Signals: do they come back four weeks in a row, and would 40% be "very disappointed" to lose it?
3. **First expansion: Bedrijf**, as the business side of the same wallet — tax reserve, private withdrawals, private + business in one overview. No invoicing, no VAT filing, no tax advice.
4. **Features, not products:** contract end dates and cancel reminders on fixed costs (the start of Administratie); kids' jars (the start of Mensen).
5. **Later, gated by retention:** Energie, Mindset as full products; Administratie and Mensen as their own tabs only if the features get used.

## 6. Not verified

Free→paid conversion and churn for Dutch budgeting apps; counts of budget coaches; aggregator prices (need quotes); a JARS trademark at EUIPO/USPTO; any name against trademark registers; whether pure budget coaching falls outside the Wft; demand for a combined private + business planner.

## 7. Decisions for Given

- Launch scope: one sharp product first, or the full map?
- First target customer: zzp households, or households in general?
- Names in §4 (Mindset is decided; the rest are proposals).
- Bank sync: get aggregator quotes now, or after validation?
- Practice: channel through independent coaches, or keep selling to organisations?
