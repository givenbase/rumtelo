# Audience

Rumtelo is for people who take their life seriously — not only those already “winning,” and not only those in crisis.

---

## Three audiences, one product

| Audience | What they feel | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Successful people** | They earn well, but money and life still feel scattered. Success without a clear picture is expensive noise. | Calm control. Direction. Every amount (and hour) already assigned. |
| **People who want change** | Something isn’t working — spending, energy, focus, or the sense that life is deciding for them. | A clear next system, not more guilt or complicated advice. |
| **People who want to be successful — but don’t know exactly what to do** | Ambition without a map. They care, they try, but income, expenses, and priorities stay fuzzy. | Intention before chaos: a simple structure for where things go — and where they should go next. |

---

## And the people who help them: practices

Coaches and advisors who help other households: budget and money coaches, debt-help and social-work teams, financial advisors, life coaches. They buy **Rumtelo Practice**: one desk to follow every client's board, only with the client's consent.

| Audience | What they feel | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Practices** | Sessions start with collecting numbers; clients lose the thread between sessions; every client lives in a different spreadsheet. | A current picture per client before the session, a routine clients keep alone, consent-first visibility, one caseload overview. |

Full positioning, pricing and what to fix before selling: [practice.md](./practice.md).

---

## Positioning

**Built for people who are doing well — and for people who are ready to.**  
**For practices:** *Stop wondering where your clients stand.*

We do **not** speak only to “struggling” or only to “elite.”  
We speak to people who want **direction**.

---

## Language & market

- **Audience start:** the owner and friends first; subscription product if it works.
- **Language:** English first, Dutch second.
- **Market:** NL / EU-first (Amsterdam hosting, EU-resident data).

---

## What we never assume about them

- That they have time to become accountants.
- That their income and expenses are stable.
- That shame or complexity will motivate them.
- That a raw bank balance is the truth.

See [research/money-awareness.md](../research/money-awareness.md) for why.
