# Brand positioning

## Name

**Rumtelo** — from Spanish *rumbo*, “course / heading” (with a distinctive *t*).  
Chosen because it means **direction**, which is what the product does.

`.com` and `.nl` were verified free at choosing (verify with **whois**, never DNS alone). Before further commitment: BOIP (Benelux) and EUIPO trademark search, classes 36 and 42.

---

## North star

**Stop wondering where it went.** ← the key (keep mysterious)

Decode sparingly (Why / L1 / landing hero only): *Wondering what? All of it — money, life, energy, time.*  
Then clarify: when you know where it went, you get **grip** again (control).  

Products are tools for parts of that grip (they can grow): *Grip on your money / growth / energy / why.* Money and Growth are the most ready today. See [explain scripts](../explain/README.md).

Do **not** repeat the decode on every screen — slogan → decode (once) → grip → product.

### Two front doors

| Door | Decode | Product |
|---|---|---|
| **People** | *Wondering what? All of it — money, life, energy, time.* | Household board: Money · Growth · Energy · Soul |
| **Practices** | *Stop wondering where your clients stand.* | Rumtelo Practice: one desk, every client's board, only with their yes |

Same key, different decode. Details: [product/practice.md](../product/practice.md).

### Naming: The Coach vs. a coach

- **The Coach** (capital T) is the in-app feature: small, rule-based tips with one next move.
- A **coach / advisor** (lowercase) is a human professional, usually working through a **Practice**.
- Never write practice copy that suggests The Coach replaces the human. *The Coach tips between sessions. You guide the decisions.*
- Use **routine** for the weekly habit, never "practice" (NL: *routine*, not *praktijk*).

## Voice

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Direct, punchy, product-clear | Soft abstraction (“clarity”, “ambition”, “built to decide”) |
| Name the problem (mystery spending / where it went) | Shame, panic, “you’re bad with money” |
| Intention, assign, picture | Luxury flex, crypto hype, neon fintech |
| Information + one next move | Alarm theatre without a CTA |

**Tone:** strong and clear, still calm — not hustle, not soft fluff.

## Visual direction (product tokens)

Source: **Complete Brand Guidelines RB** (logo, palette, typography).

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Accent | `#06656C` brand teal | Primary actions, Freedom jar, growth |
| Accent tint | `#00777F` → `#2EAEAA` → `#4ED9D2` | Hover / dark-mode accents |
| Ink | `#1A202D` brand navy | Primary text / dark surfaces |
| Surfaces | `#EEF1F5` / `#FFFFFF` | Cool wash + white cards |
| Display font | Archivo Narrow (web stand-in for Tungsten) | Headlines |
| Body | Archivo | UI copy (14–19pt on web) |
| Mono | Plex Mono | Labels, rituals, meta |
| Logo mark | Teal gradient `#27A19A` → `#0D656C` | Stylized R + lowercase wordmark |

**Avoid:** purple/indigo as brand identity, neon, crypto gold, loud bank-blue gradients. Do not recolor or stretch the logo lockup.

**Logo preference:** combination mark (gradient R + `umtelo` wordmark) + lettermark **R** favicon.  
Metaphors: jars/vessels, direction, quiet geometric glyph. **Not** pigs, coins, €/$, charts.
