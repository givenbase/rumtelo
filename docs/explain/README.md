# Explain videos — large → small

Scripts for avatar videos (and Why / Help). Same story everywhere. **Short. Clear. Beginner-friendly.**

| Level | What it answers | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | What is Rumtelo? (no product list) | Why opening, Help general |
| **L2** | What does this product do? | Why card, Help for that product |
| **L3** | What does this screen do? | Help on that screen (+ tour) |
| **L1-P** | What is Rumtelo for a practice? | Practice desk Help, sales follow-up, website practice section |

**L1 stays general.** Products can grow. Don’t list them all in the main video. Money and Growth are the most ready today; other products get their own L2 when ready.

## The idea in plain words

1. **Slogan (the key):** *Stop wondering where it went.*  
2. **Decode once (L1 / Why / landing hero only):** Wondering what? → *All of it* — money, life, energy, time.  
3. **Then grip:** when you know where it went, you get **grip** again (control).  
4. **L2 products:** same story — this tool is grip on money / growth / energy / why. The Coach tips; **you** choose.

Not a budget app. Not something that decides for you.

## Writing rules

- Short sentences
- Everyday words (no “autopilot”, “captain”, “reins”, “systems” overload)
- One idea per line
- EN first, NL twin
- End with: you decide

## Length

| Level | About |
|---|---|
| L1 | 45–60s |
| L2 | 35–45s |
| L3 | 25–35s |
